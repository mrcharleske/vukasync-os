# Client Lifecycle

This document outlines the expected client lifecycle that VukaSync OS should
support.

## 1. Acquisition and signup

Clients may enter the platform through:

- Self-service signup and payment.
- Administrator-created accounts.
- Custom service arrangements.

The system should support both automated provisioning and manual onboarding.

## 2. Onboarding

Onboarding may include:

- Client organization setup.
- User invitations.
- Service assignment.
- Subscription or custom arrangement setup.
- Initial requirements collection.
- File and asset intake.
- Scheduling a kickoff meeting.

## 3. Active delivery

During active delivery, clients should be able to see:

- Project progress.
- Activities and updates.
- Milestones.
- Reports.
- Files and deliverables.
- Support tickets.
- Meeting links and notes where appropriate.

Internal team members should be able to add structured updates, private notes,
and client-visible commentary.

## 4. Reporting

Reports should combine:

- Automated metrics where integrations are available.
- Manual analysis and recommendations.
- Progress against agreed goals.
- Clear next steps.

Reports may require staff review before publishing.

## 5. Support and renewal

The platform should support:

- Ongoing support tickets.
- Resource access.
- Subscription renewal or plan changes.
- Manual account review.
- Continued service expansion into additional modules.
